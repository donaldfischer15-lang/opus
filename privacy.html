Opus privacy

Opus is a personal app used by one person, its owner. It has no other users and no accounts.

  • Data from connected services, such as WHOOP, is read only by the owner's own helper and saved in the owner's private repository.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, used for advertising, or used to train any AI model.
  • The owner can disconnect a service at any time, which stops all further access, and can delete the saved data.

Questions go to the owner directly.
